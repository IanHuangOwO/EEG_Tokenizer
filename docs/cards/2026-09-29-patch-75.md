# Card: patch length 75 (tiny corpus, 3 pretrain seeds x 3 finetune seeds)

Written 2026-09-29, before any patch-75 run trained. Follows the patch-length card (50 vs 100: patch 100
won MI few-shot, 004 +5.7 / 001 +3.7, and lost P300 loso, -1.9).

- **Change:** `patch_len` / `patch_length` 75, `patch_stride` 37 (0.375 s patches, ~51% overlap; 26 patches tile
  the 5 s pretraining window exactly; finetune windows drop <= 22 end samples, BNCI2014008's 1 s trials become
  4 patches covering 0-0.93 s). Everything else as the patch-50/100 runs: static 16 stamps, same tiny corpus,
  seeds 1-3 (`mesae_tiny_p75_s16_s1..3`). Compared against the six existing backbones of the patch-length card.
- **Hypothesis:** a middle length keeps most of the MI few-shot gain (2.7 Hz bins, most of a mu cycle per token)
  without the P300 loss (4 patches per 1 s trial instead of 3).

## Metrics

1. **Primary -- downstream z probe**, same protocol and win rule as the patch-length card (6 cells, finetune seeds
   1-3; per patch length mean +- SE over its 3 pretrain seeds; won when |diff| > 2 x sqrt(SE_a^2 + SE_b^2)).
2. **Decision:** patch 75 becomes the default if, against patch 50, it wins >= 1 cell and loses none. Otherwise
   patch 50 stays (the P300 cost of longer patches is then unavoidable at this corpus size).
3. **Secondary:** patch 75 vs patch 100 on the MI few-shot cells (share of patch 100's gain kept); ridge probe on
   z; masked reconstruction reported, not judged (masks are defined in patches).

Note: the patch-75 pretrains use the recompiled STEW / Weibo2014 / Inria caches, like patch-50 seeds 2-3.

## Result (2026-09-29 12:34): patch 50 stays

Against patch 50, patch 75 wins BNCI2014004 loso (+3.0, threshold 2.6) and few-shot (+2.8, 2.7) and loses
BNCI2014008 loso (-0.7, threshold 0.1): the rule (>= 1 win, no loss) fails. Decision (user): patch 50 stays the
default. Patch 75 keeps most of patch 100's MI gain at a third of its P300 cost and has the best average rank
among all Compass loso entries (7.0 vs 8.3). Full tables: docs/reports/2026-09-29-patch-75.md.

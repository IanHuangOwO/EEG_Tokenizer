# Patch length 75, and where patch 50 / 75 / 100 land against EEG-FM-Compass

- **Date:** 2026-09-29
- **Question:** does a 0.375 s patch keep patch 100's MI gain without its P300 loss; and how do the three patch
  lengths rank against the published foundation models under the same loso protocol?
- **Runs:** `mesae_tiny_p75_s16_s1..3` (patch 75, stride 37), against the patch-50 and patch-100 seeds of the
  patch-length report. Static 16 stamps, tiny corpus, only patch geometry and training seed differ.
- **Protocol:** z probe (`latent_signed`, pca, spatial_k 8), 6 cells, finetune seeds 1-3; per patch length mean +-
  SE over 3 pretrain seeds; won when |diff| > 2 x sqrt(SE_a^2 + SE_b^2). Compass: Table V, cross-subject.
- **Verdict:** patch 75 wins BNCI2014004 loso (+3.0) and few-shot (+2.8) against patch 50 but loses BNCI2014008
  loso (-0.7, threshold 0.1), so the registered rule keeps patch 50. **Decision (user, 2026-09-29): patch 50
  stays the default.** Under loso all three lengths rank 3rd / 3rd / 1st-2nd among Compass's 13 frozen-backbone
  linear probes on BNCI2014001 / 004 / 008.
- **Cards / ADRs:** [patch-75 card](../cards/2026-09-29-patch-75.md), [patch-length report](2026-09-29-patch-length.md);
  ADR 0020; docs/finetune-caveats.md items 7-9.

## Notes

- Why patch 50 despite patch 75's better overall rank: the registered rule (no cell lost) fails on P300, patch 50
  keeps the finer time resolution ERP decoding needs, and a smoothing benefit for MI can still be had in the head
  (test A, `latent_pool`) without retraining the backbone. The 004 gain is also the kind of MI gain whose source is
  unresolved (fewer probe inputs, or a smoother read of the cue response; caveats 7-8).
- The P300 threshold (0.1) is small because the three patch-50 seeds scored almost identically on 008; it counts
  pretrain-seed spread only, not finetune-seed noise (up to ~0.4).
- Compass comparability: same sessions, loso and balanced accuracy; our metric is the mean of the last 10 epochs
  (Compass: last epoch), and our tiny corpus is ~10 h of windows against thousands of hours for their models.

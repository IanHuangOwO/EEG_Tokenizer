# Spatial encoding and masking: base, A, B, AB

- **Date:** 2026-09-26
- **Question:** do the mixture masking curriculum (A) and the spatial encoding (B) improve the backbone and
  downstream accuracy, alone and together?
- **Runs** (tiny corpus, static 16 stamps, archived in `output/archive/`):
  - base `mesae_tiny_static16_base_s1`: `random_to_complementary` masking, no coordinate encoding
  - A `mesae_tiny_static16_groupA_s1`: `mixture` masking (channel_cluster 0.35 / random_channel 0.35 /
    time_block 0.30, ramp 10 epochs) plus channel subsampling to sparse montages (prob 0.2)
  - B `mesae_tiny_static16_groupB_s1`: Fourier coordinate encoding plus directional spatial bias
  - AB `mesae_tiny_static16_groupAB_s1`: both (`spatial_embedding: true`)
- **Protocol:** one pretrain seed each. Downstream: learned stamp head (`frozen_learned`), tail balanced
  accuracy, frozen protocols mi_loso / mi_fewshot / p300_loso / p300_fewshot (tuned on DEV sets only),
  paired over subjects, Holm over 18 tests. Tie band +-3 points, clear gain >= +2. Backbone: masked MSE on
  512 held-out pretrain windows, identical masks; "x IDW" = model / best model-free interpolation.
- **Verdict:** AB is the only variant with a clear key-cell gain (+2.2 on BNCI2014001 loso) and it stacks
  (mean +0.7 vs A +0.5, B -0.0); every variant beats IDW on whole-channel masks and improves sparse-to-dense
  imputation by >= 5%. AB became the recipe. No downstream difference survives Holm correction.
- **Cards / ADRs:** predates the card system (ADR 0020); hypotheses were fixed before the results.

## Notes

- Single pretrain seed per variant; downstream differences are within the per-subject CIs except
  BNCI2014004 loso for AB (a loss, not significant after Holm).
- Downstream used the old event-anchored trial windows (before the EEG-FM-Compass windows).

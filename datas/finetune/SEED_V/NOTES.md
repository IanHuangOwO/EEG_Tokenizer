# SEED-V -- gated, not fetched (2026-09-24)

SJTU multimodal emotion dataset (BCMI Lab), EEG-FM-Bench member. 16 subjects,
3 sessions x 15 movie clips, 5 emotions (happy / sad / fear / disgust /
neutral), 62ch ESI NeuroScan, 1000Hz native (plus eye-movement data).

5-class. Split is subject-DEPENDENT: the 15 trials per session are divided
1:1:1 into train/val/test.

Access: same process as SEED (bcmi.sjtu.edu.cn/home/seed/seed-v.html) --
application form + license agreement signed by a faculty member, emailed to
the lab; download link sent after approval. No public mirror.

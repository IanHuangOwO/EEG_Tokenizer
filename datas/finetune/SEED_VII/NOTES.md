80 video clips over 4 sessions, 7 emotions (happy / sad / fear / disgust /
neutral / anger / surprise), 62ch ESI NeuroScan, 1000Hz native.

appendix lists SEED-VII this way, although the main text calls all SEED-family
splits subject-dependent -- check their repo's config before reproducing).

application form + license agreement, download link sent after approval.
